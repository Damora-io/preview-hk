What we collect

When you send an inquiry: what you're looking for, the details you choose to give (piece, metal, stones, budget, dates, notes, reference photos), your name and the contact details you enter, and your viewing preference. Each answer is saved as you give it, so an unfinished inquiry still reaches HK.

Why

To answer your inquiry, quote your piece and arrange your viewing. Nothing else. Your details are never sold.

The VIP drop list

Only if you tick the box. You'll get texts and emails about new drops from HKDIAMONDZ & CO, Toronto. Every message tells you how to unsubscribe, and you can ask to be removed at any time.

Viewings

For in-person viewings HK checks photo ID before sharing the location. ID is checked, not copied or kept HK to confirm.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted. Contact HK's contact for privacy requests.
