The HK Promise

Know exactly what you're buying.

Buying jewelry online comes down to trust. Here is what HK puts in writing on every piece.

On every piece

  • A stone labelNatural diamond, lab-grown diamond or moissanite. "Lab-grown" always sits right before "diamond", the same size. "Diamond" on its own means natural.
  • Metal, karat and gram weightOn the card and on your receipt.
  • Total carat weightWhere the stones are weighed, the total is written down.
  • Certificates where they existGIA or IGI report numbers shown with the piece. Not every stone has one; when it doesn't, the card says so.
  • Real photos and videoEvery piece for sale is shown in HK's own photos and video, never a computer image.

On every custom order

  • A written quote firstPrice, stones, metal, deposit and delivery date before any work starts.
  • Video from the benchUpdates while your piece is made.
  • A reply timeHK answers inquiries within HK to confirm.
  • Traceable paymentsCard deposits with bank checks built in. A receipt for every payment.

After you buy

Warranty HK to confirm. Resizing, upgrades and trade-ins HK to confirm. Returns on custom pieces HK to confirm.

Customised watches and eyewear

Some timepieces and frames are customised by HK with added diamonds. Each listing says what is original and what HK added. A customised piece is not sold as a new product of its original maker, the maker's warranty may not apply after customisation, and HKDIAMONDZ & CO is not affiliated with any watch or eyewear brand.

Private by design

There is no address on this site. The location is shared once a viewing is confirmed, and photo ID is checked first. It keeps the pieces, the buyers and HK safe.
