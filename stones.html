Natural, lab-grown or moissanite?

Three stones end up in iced-out jewelry. They can look alike from across the room. Here's how they differ, plainly.

The short answer

What "VVS" means

VVS is a clarity grade: "very, very slightly included". The flaws are so small a trained grader needs magnification to find them. It describes clarity only. A VVS stone can be natural, lab-grown or moissanite, so ask which one.

How HK labels stones

Every piece names its stone: natural diamond, lab-grown diamond or moissanite. "Lab-grown" always sits right before "diamond". "Diamond" on its own means natural. Where a stone has a GIA or IGI report, the number is shown with the piece.

Which one is right for you

It depends on the piece, the size and what it means to you. Tell HK your idea and budget, choose "Help me choose" in the inquiry, and you'll get an honest answer.
