The HK Promise

Know exactly what you're buying.

  • A stone label on every pieceNatural diamond, lab-grown diamond or moissanite. Named every time, same size as the word "diamond".
  • Metal, karat and gram weightWritten on the card and on your receipt, so the piece is never smaller than the photos.
  • Certificates where they existGIA or IGI report numbers shown with the piece.
  • Made on cameraVideo from the bench while your custom piece is set.
