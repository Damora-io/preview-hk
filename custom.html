How it works

  1. The ideaSend a sketch, a screenshot or just the words. Initials, a name, a symbol, a photo of someone. HK asks the questions that matter: size, metal, stones, budget, date.
  2. The designHK draws it up as a sketch or a render, so you see the piece before any metal is cast.
  3. Approval and a written quotePrice, stone type, metal and karat, the deposit and the delivery date, in writing. Nothing starts until you approve it.
  4. Setting at the benchThe piece is cast, then the stones are set by hand. You get video updates while it's made.
  5. DeliveryPicked up at a private viewing or delivered. It comes with its stone label, metal, karat and gram weight in writing, and the certificate where there is one.

Turnaround and deposit

Turnaround HK to confirm depends on the size of the piece and the stones. Custom work starts with a deposit HK to confirm after the written quote, paid by card with bank checks built in, so it's traceable for you and for HK.

Questions people ask
